1. Information We Access

To provide its ghost-hunting and paranormal investigation features, the App may request access to the following on your device:

  • Microphone audio — captured during use of the Spirit Box and EVP Recorder features.
  • Camera feed — captured during use of the Infrared Camera feature.
  • App usage data — such as which features you open and session activity, used only to run the App itself (e.g. displaying your session time and dashboard stats).

We do not request access to your contacts, location, phone call data, SMS messages, photos/media outside what you actively capture in-app, or any other device data not listed above.

2. How We Use This Information

All microphone and camera data captured within the App is processed locally on your device to power in-app features, including:

  • Simulated "spirit response" audio analysis within the Spirit Box
  • Recording and playback within the EVP Recorder
  • Live visual filters and AI-style anomaly overlays within the Infrared Camera
  • Session statistics shown on your Dashboard (e.g. session time, activity levels)

We do not use this data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the App's core features to you.

3. Data Storage & Retention

Recordings, captured images, and session logs created within the App are stored locally on your device only, for as long as you choose to keep them. You may delete this data at any time through your device's app storage settings or, where available, in-app controls. We do not maintain remote servers that store your recordings or camera captures.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not currently integrate third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs into the App. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated in advance of the change taking effect, along with the App's Play Store listing.

The App is distributed via Google Play, and your download, installation, and payment (if applicable) is subject to Google's own Privacy Policy as the platform provider.

6. Security

Because audio and camera data are processed and stored locally on your device rather than transmitted to external servers, the primary security safeguard is your device's own lock screen and storage protections. We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date and using a secure lock method (PIN, password, or biometric) to protect any locally stored recordings.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Access the information the App has generated on your device (recordings, logs)
  • Delete that information at any time via your device
  • Withdraw microphone or camera permission at any time via device settings
  • Uninstall the App, which removes all locally stored App data from your device

South African users are protected under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Because the App does not transmit personal data to us or any third party, there is no remotely held personal information for us to provide, correct, or erase on your behalf beyond what is described above.

8. International Users

The App may be downloaded by users outside South Africa. Since all sensitive data (audio, camera) is processed locally on-device and not transmitted to us, no cross-border data transfer of this information occurs through our systems.
