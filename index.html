ENTERTAINMENT PURPOSES ONLY

Ecchos of the Past is a novelty and entertainment app. Its spirit box, EMF readings, EVP tools, radar, geophone, and AI analysis are simulated or use ordinary phone sensors and audio synthesis for atmosphere. Nothing in this app is scientifically validated evidence of paranormal activity, and results should not be treated as fact.

PHOTOSENSITIVITY WARNING

This app uses sudden flashing red effects, strobing visuals, and jump-style audio cues. If you or anyone using this app has photosensitive epilepsy, a seizure disorder, migraines, or anxiety triggered by flashing lights or sudden loud sounds, do not use this app, or use it with flash and sound effects disabled where possible.

PHYSICAL SAFETY

  • Never use this app while driving, operating machinery, or in any situation requiring your full attention.
  • If investigating a real location, you are solely responsible for your own physical safety. Watch for trip hazards, unstable structures, low light, stairs, uneven ground, and other risks, especially while looking at your screen.
  • Do not trespass. You are responsible for obtaining permission to be on any property or cemetery you investigate, and for following local laws about disturbing burial sites.
  • Do not dig, disturb, or physically interact with any suspected grave site. The Graveyard tool is for recording locations only.
  • Do not use this app in any way that could put yourself or others at risk of injury.

MENTAL AND EMOTIONAL WELLBEING

This app is designed to be spooky and immersive. If you are prone to anxiety, fear-based distress, or find paranormal-themed content upsetting, please use caution or avoid the app. Not recommended for young children without adult supervision.

CAMERA AND MICROPHONE

Camera and microphone access are optional and only used live, on-device, to power the visual and audio tools while you use them. Nothing is uploaded by this app. Community features only store the text you choose to submit.
